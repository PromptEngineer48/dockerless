# Your reference voice goes here

- `ref.wav` — 5–15 seconds of you speaking. Clean recording, one speaker,
  no background music. WAV/16-bit preferred (MP3 also works).
- `ref.txt` — the exact transcript of `ref.wav`, word for word.

Qwen3-TTS uses this pair to clone your voice for the narration.
Tip: speak with the energy you want the video to have — the clone copies
your delivery, not just your timbre.
